How We Vet · The Talent Engine

AI has made fake engineering profiles cheap.

Fintech never opens an account on trust. Hiring shouldn’t either.

A polished résumé, a matching LinkedIn, and a confident first call no longer prove that a person exists, or that they did the work. So every candidate passes five gates before you ever see a profile. We call it Know Your Workforce: identity verification as a hiring standard.

Five Gates

Five gates before a candidate reaches you.

  1. Gate 01

    Fraud-aware screening

    Every résumé is cross-checked against LinkedIn history, GitHub, and public work, with tooling that flags mismatched experience.

  2. Gate 02

    Identity verification

    A formal ID check through an international verification vendor: document scanning, database validation, tamper detection. 100% of candidates, before any client interview.

  3. Gate 03

    Technical evaluation

    Hands-on skill and AI-native ability are tested, not assumed, including a live build in under 45 minutes with the candidate’s own models and agents. Senior means 6+ years.

  4. Gate 04

    Recorded English assessment

    Architecture and engineering decisions explained fluently in English, on video. The profile is client-ready, or it isn’t presented.

  5. Gate 05

    The hire bar

    One last question, asked about every candidate: “Would we hire this person for our team?”

<0.4%

of applicants are accepted. Senior+ bar.

You still run the final interviews.

The five gates decide who you meet. Your standards decide who joins. Background checks run in parallel with technical matching, through a standardized pre-employment due diligence process applied consistently and without exception.

Serious engineers welcome verification, because it protects them too: every fake profile makes the next honest engineer look a little more suspicious.

Open Source

We published the checks.

Our identity-verification skill is free and MIT licensed. It checks two moments: before the first interview (résumé consistency, profile age and connections, code-history and time-zone patterns, references) and before an offer (government and tax IDs for Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, and the US, proof of address, and whether it all tells the same story). Each candidate lands in a risk tier: Low, Medium, High, or Block.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you detect AI interview fraud and fake engineering profiles?

Résumés are cross-checked against LinkedIn history, GitHub and public work, with tooling that flags mismatched experience. Then every candidate passes a formal identity check through an international verification vendor before any client interview.

Are your engineers ID-verified?

Yes. 100% of candidates pass identity verification, including document scanning, database validation and tamper detection, before any client-facing interview.

How do you test AI skills?

AI-native ability is tested, not assumed. Candidates do a live build in under 45 minutes using their own models and agents, alongside a technical evaluation of their hands-on experience.

What does senior mean at Icalia Labs?

Mid-level means 4 to 6 years of hands-on experience; senior means 6 or more. Fewer than 0.4% of applicants are accepted.

Do we still interview the engineers?

Yes. You still run the final interviews. Candidates who aren't client-ready are not presented.

Do you run background checks?

Yes. We apply a standardized pre-employment due diligence process, consistently and without exception, in parallel with technical matching.
