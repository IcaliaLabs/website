FAQ

## Frequently asked questions

### Which engagement model is right for us?

Ask who should manage the engineers day to day. If you want to change how your team builds with AI, start with Agentic Engineering. If you need senior capacity that stays and you'll direct the work, use Talent Augmentation. If you want to own the team outright, use Placements or build-operate-transfer.

### What is Talent Augmentation?

Talent Augmentation is our managed nearshore staff augmentation. Senior engineers from Mexico and Latin America join your team full time and work your hours. You run the final interviews and direct the work; we handle employment, payroll, growth plans and continuity.

### What is build-operate-transfer?

We build the team, operate it with you until it runs on its own, and then transfer the engineers to your company. It suits teams of 5 to 10 or more people and works a bit like a small acquisition: you see the team deliver before you take it over.

### Do you offer Employer of Record?

Yes. Placements combine recruitment with Employer of Record, at cost-plus, for companies that want to hire engineers in Latin America and manage them directly.

### How do you bill?

Engagements bill time and materials, usually on an evergreen SOW so there are fewer renewal cycles to administer. One relationship, one invoice.
