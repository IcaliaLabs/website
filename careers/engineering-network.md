## About the role

The best engineers are already building — designing systems, shipping products, solving hard problems, and raising the technical bar around them. At Icalia, we connect experienced engineers across Latin America with US and global product teams working on meaningful technical challenges. If you're considering what's next, tell us what you build, the technologies you know deeply, the systems you've worked on, and the kind of problems you want to solve. We look beyond job titles and keywords, because the right match isn't just about the stack — it's about the engineer and the problem.

## What you'll do

-   Introduce yourself once: share what you build, the technologies you know deeply, the systems you've worked on, and the problems you want to solve.
-   Get matched on technical relevance — opportunities aligned with your actual experience and strengths, not keyword filters.
-   Do real engineering work alongside product and engineering teams building, scaling, and improving real software.
-   Hear from us directly when there's a reason to talk — no black-box ATS and no endless applications.

## What we're looking for

-   Professional experience building and shipping production software
-   Technical depth in the technologies and systems you work with
-   Strong engineering judgment and a sense of ownership over outcomes
-   Ability to operate independently within distributed product teams
-   Based in Latin America and comfortable working with US and global teams

## Our engineering values.

The same standards every Icalia engineer is held to — and the reason our clients keep our people through acquisitions.

### Craft & ownership

We write code we'd be proud to sign. You own outcomes end to end — well-tested, observable, and built to outlast the sprint that shipped it.

### Async, remote discipline

Same time zone as your team, clear written communication, and the autonomy to move without being managed. Our rituals are why clients call our process world-class.

### Mentorship & growth

Code reviews that teach, documented patterns, and senior engineers who lift the bench. You'll grow faster here than on most in-house teams.

### AI-augmented, human-accountable

We use modern AI tooling to multiply output — but a named human always owns the quality, security, and judgment behind every line that ships.
