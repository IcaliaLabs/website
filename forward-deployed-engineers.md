FAQ

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a nearshore forward deployed engineer?

A nearshore forward deployed engineer (FDE) is a senior engineer from a nearby, time-zone-aligned country who works embedded in the client's team and owns outcomes in production, not just assigned tickets.

### How is a forward deployed engineer different from staff augmentation?

Staff augmentation adds capacity to a backlog. A forward deployed engineer is accountable for what reaches production: they learn the business and its constraints, shape the work with your leads, and build with AI agents inside a reviewed workflow. Our Talent Augmentation contracts are how FDEs usually join a team.

### Can a forward deployed engineer be fractional?

Yes. Senior experts can join for 15 to 20 hours a week as part of an Agentic Engineering engagement, or full time when the work needs a dedicated owner.

### What time zone do your engineers work in?

US hours. Our engineers are in Mexico and Latin America, and Monterrey runs on Central Standard Time year-round, so standups, reviews and incidents happen inside your working day.

### How fast can a forward deployed engineer start?

We present matched candidate profiles within days. You run the final interviews, and the engineer joins your workflows and tools from day one.

### Who manages a forward deployed engineer day to day?

You direct the work alongside your leads. We handle employment, growth plans, performance reviews and continuity behind them, so the knowledge stays with your team.
